## Primary check (from the wave table)

> Dual-Family member switches Families — Tower, streak, Vow holder all switch with zero bleed.

All steps use the QA fixtures, not the isolation fixtures. `dual@` is in **QA
Family A** and **QA Family B**, and the two are seeded deliberately different —
identical content would let every step below pass while proving nothing.

1. Log in as `dual@f4milia.test`. You land on **QA Family A**.
   **Expect:** the page header reads *QA Family A*. Six sections are present:
   Today at the Table, The Tower, Your Bricks in the left column; The Vow,
   Streak, The Ledger in the right rail.
2. Look at **The Tower**.
   **Expect:** the title is *Finish the allotment shed*, on a dark panel. Below
   it, progress is drawn as a **wall of small blocks**, not a bar. Some blocks
   are lit, the rest dimmed.
3. Look at the line under the blocks.
   **Expect:** it reads `1 / 3 Bricks laid`. Count the blocks: there are **24**,
   and roughly a third are lit. The number is Bricks, not Builds.
4. Look at **The Vow**.
   **Expect:** *I will water the plot on Wednesdays*, and `HELD BY` a name that
   is **not** Dual Family. A small square sits beside the word `ACTIVE` — the
   word must be there, not just the square.
5. Look at **Streak**.
   **Expect:** a large terracotta **3**, then `DAYS AT THE TABLE`, then the line
   about a missed day holding the streak.
6. Look at **Your Bricks**.
   **Expect:** an honest empty state — *Nothing claimed. Open Bricks are on the
   Family board.* `dual@` holds no Bricks in this Family.
7. Look at **The Ledger**.
   **Expect:** at least one entry, each with a monospace type-and-date line
   above it.
8. Switch to **QA Family B** using the Family picker at the top of the sidebar.
   **Expect:** the header changes to *QA Family B*.
9. **The switch is the point of this session.** Compare against steps 2–5.
   **Expect:** the Tower is now *Run the winter supper club*. The Vow is *I will
   book the hall by the 20th*, held by **Dual Family**. The streak is **1**, not 3.
10. Still on QA Family B, read the whole page carefully.
    **Expect:** **nothing** from QA Family A appears anywhere — no *allotment
    shed*, no *water the plot on Wednesdays*, no Ledger line about the shed.
    This is the zero-bleed check; anything leaking here is a fail, not a nit.
11. Go back to QA Family A.
    **Expect:** step 2–5 values return exactly. Nothing from Family B persists.
